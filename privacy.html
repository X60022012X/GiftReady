Privacy Policy

GiftReady is designed with privacy in mind. Your birthdays, gift ideas, notes, and gift status stay under your control.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

👤 Information stored in GiftReady

GiftReady allows you to enter information about people you want to remember birthdays and gifts for.

  • Names
  • Birthdays
  • Gift ideas
  • Gift purchase status
  • Notes you choose to add

📱 Data storage and iCloud

The information you enter into GiftReady is stored on your device using Apple's storage technologies.

GiftReady uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to store and synchronize your data across your Apple devices.

GiftReady does not operate its own server and does not receive or independently store the information you enter into the app.

📊 Data collection

GiftReady's developer does not collect the personal information you enter into the app.

GiftReady does not currently use analytics services, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or data brokers.

Your GiftReady data may be processed and stored by Apple as part of the iCloud synchronization service.

🤝 Data sharing

GiftReady does not sell, rent, or share the information you enter into the app for advertising, marketing, or data-broker purposes.

Data may be transmitted to and stored by Apple as necessary to provide iCloud synchronization.

🔔 Notifications

GiftReady may ask for permission to send local notifications. These notifications are used to remind you about upcoming birthdays and gifts.

You can change notification permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

🗑️ Data deletion

You can delete individual people and their associated information directly inside GiftReady.

Deletions are synchronized through iCloud to your other devices.

Deleting GiftReady from a device removes the app's locally stored data from that device. Data synchronized with iCloud may remain in iCloud or on your other devices after the app is deleted from a single device.

🔗 Third-party services

GiftReady does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

GiftReady uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to provide data storage and synchronization.

The use of iCloud and CloudKit is subject to Apple's applicable privacy practices and terms.

👶 Children's privacy

GiftReady does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Information entered into GiftReady is stored on the user's device and may also be stored and synchronized through the user's personal iCloud account.

📝 Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if GiftReady's features or data practices change.

The latest version of the policy will always be available on this page.
