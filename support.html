Support

Need help with GiftReady? Find answers to common questions below, or contact us if you need additional support.

🎂 Birthday reminders aren't appearing

Make sure notifications are enabled for GiftReady in your iPhone's Settings. Open Settings → Notifications → GiftReady and make sure Allow Notifications is enabled.

🎁 How do gift reminders work?

GiftReady reminds you before an upcoming birthday so you have time to find and buy a gift. Once you've marked a gift as bought, GiftReady will stop sending gift reminders for that birthday.

🗑️ How do I delete someone?

Open the person you want to remove and select Delete person. Their birthday, gift information, and notes will be removed from GiftReady.

🔒 Where is my information stored?

GiftReady stores your information locally on your device. For more information about how GiftReady handles your data, read our Privacy Policy.

✉️ Still need help?

If you're experiencing a problem with GiftReady or have a question that isn't answered above, feel free to contact us.
